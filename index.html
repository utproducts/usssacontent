You may request removal of your content from USSSA's platforms by contacting USSSA directly. USSSA will make reasonable efforts to remove content upon request, though content that has already been shared or redistributed by third parties may remain accessible.

Personal information collected through this platform (name, email, phone) will be used solely for the purpose of managing content submissions and contacting you regarding featured content. Your information will not be sold to third parties.
