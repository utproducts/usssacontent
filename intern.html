Program Details

Everything you need to know about the internship.

📍 Location

Space Coast Complex — Melbourne, Florida
This is an on-site position. You'll be working across the complex filming live events, practices, and everything in between.

💰 Compensation

This is an unpaid internship focused on building real-world experience. You'll gain hands-on skills in sports content creation, professional portfolio material, and direct mentorship from the USSSA content team — experience that sets you apart in the industry.

🌟 What You'll Do

  • Receive a daily shot list with specific video references and assignments
  • Film content across the Space Coast Complex (games, highlights, interviews, b-roll)
  • Create short-form content for USSSA's social media accounts (Reels, TikToks, stories)
  • Label, name, and organize all footage using our file management system
  • Submit completed content for review and publishing
  • Collaborate with the USSSA content team on creative direction

✅ What We're Looking For

  • Passion for sports content and social media
  • Experience shooting video on a phone or camera (show us your work!)
  • Familiarity with short-form content (Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts)
  • Reliable, organized, and ready to work in a fast-paced environment
  • Ability to be on-site at the Space Coast Complex in Melbourne, FL
