What We Look For

More Than Stats.
Real Character.

The USSSA Player of the Week isn't just the best hitter or the hottest arm. We're looking for the complete picture — a young athlete who makes everyone around them better.

01 🏅
Role Model

Demonstrates exceptional sportsmanship, respects opponents, coaches, and umpires, and carries themselves with integrity in every situation — win or lose.

02 ⭐
Unique & Interesting

Did something that made people stop and take notice — an extraordinary play, an inspirational moment, or a story that sets this player apart from everyone else.

03 🤝
Great Teammate

Puts the team first. Encourages struggling players, celebrates others' success, and elevates the energy and culture of their dugout every single day.

04 🌍
Community Impact

Makes a difference beyond the diamond — volunteering, mentoring younger players, showing up for their school or community in meaningful ways.

Beyond the Diamond
