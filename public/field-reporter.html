About the Program

Be the Story.
Not Just a Player in It.

The USSSA Field Reporter Network is an exclusive program for talented young athletes — in both baseball and fastpitch — who want to develop skills beyond the field. As a Field Reporter, you'll learn to conduct interviews, create content, tell compelling stories, and build a real media portfolio — all while representing USSSA at tournaments across the country.

This isn't just a title. It's a career-building opportunity that teaches communication, leadership, and media skills that will last a lifetime — whether you end up in professional sports, media, or any other field.

Program Requirements

What We Expect
From Our Reporters

1
Weekly Field Interview

Submit one field interview per week to maintain your active reporter status. Interviews can be with players, coaches, or tournament directors — video, audio, or written format accepted.

2
Monthly Group Meetings

Attend monthly virtual meetups with the USSSA content team and fellow reporters. These are part training, part community — you'll learn from each other and from us.

3
Professional Standards

Represent USSSA with integrity. All content must be respectful, factual, and appropriate for a youth sports audience. You are the face of the network in the field.

4
Active USSSA Membership

Must be an active USSSA player or coach in good standing throughout your time in the program. Ages 8–14 (8U through 14U) eligible to apply as a player reporter. Baseball and Fastpitch welcome.

5
Complete Onboarding

All accepted reporters complete a short onboarding training covering USSSA's content standards, interview best practices, and how to use the submission portal.

Skills You'll Develop

→ Interviewing & Active Listening — How to ask the right questions and make people comfortable on camera
→ Video Content Creation — Filming, framing, and producing short-form sports content for social media
→ Storytelling — How to find the compelling angle in any situation and present it memorably
→ Personal Branding — Building a public presence that works for you on and off the field
→ Writing & Editing — Crafting clear, engaging game summaries, features, and social captions
→ Public Speaking — On-camera confidence, presence, and delivery
→ Deadline Management — Real-world accountability with weekly submission requirements
