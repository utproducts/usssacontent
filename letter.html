A Letter They'll Never Forget

Behind every athlete is someone who believed first. We're honoring those people — one letter at a time.

Share Your Story

What would an athlete say if they had the chance to read a letter, out loud, to the person who changed everything?

Some of the most meaningful moments in sports never happen under the lights. They happen in the early mornings, in the long drives home, in the encouragement after disappointment, and in the steady support that helps an athlete keep going.

A Letter They'll Never Forget is a new USSSA storytelling series created to honor the people behind the journey. We're inviting athletes to submit a personal story and a letter to someone who shaped their path — a parent, coach, grandparent, sibling, teammate, mentor, or even their younger self.

We are starting in Texas, and we want these first stories to reflect the heart, family values, and emotional depth that define so many journeys in your community.
